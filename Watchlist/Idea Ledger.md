# Idea Ledger

What this note is for: an append-only record of every prospective idea the brief or a screen surfaces, so sourcing can be refined on real hit-rate instead of memory. One row per idea at the moment it is surfaced: never delete, only add or re-score. Paired with [[Sourcing Rules]], which I revise monthly using what this ledger shows. Prices are close-to-close, price-return only (no dividends). Not advice.

How to log a new idea: append a row with the anchor close on the surfacing date, the source bucket, a dislocation-cause tag (sentiment/multiple vs fundamental-break), whether it is in-circle, and the verdict. Re-score all open rows on the monthly review and stamp the date.

## Source buckets
- **INS** = Lane B insider-cluster screen (Form 4, clustered open-market buying). Kevin's stated edge.
- **QD** = Lane A quality-dislocation shortlist (quality franchise sold off on sentiment/multiple, incl. post-earnings overreaction).
- **SS** = other special situation (spinoff, activist, etc.).

## Dislocation-cause tags
- **SENT** = sentiment/multiple/overreaction drop, business intact.
- **BREAK** = fundamental deterioration (margins, unit economics, guidance cut on real weakness).
- **NA** = not dislocation-sourced.
