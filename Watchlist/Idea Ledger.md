# Idea Ledger

What this note is for: an append-only record of every prospective idea the brief or a screen surfaces, so sourcing can be refined on real hit-rate instead of memory. One row per idea at the moment it is surfaced: never delete, only add or re-score. Paired with [[Sourcing Rules]], which I revise monthly using what this ledger shows. Prices are close-to-close, price-return only (no dividends). Not advice.

How to log a new idea: append a row with the anchor close on the surfacing date, the source bucket, a dislocation-cause tag (sentiment/multiple vs fundamental-break), whether it is in-circle, and the verdict. Re-score all open rows on the monthly review and stamp the date.

## Source buckets
- **INS** = Lane B insider-cluster screen (Form 4, clustered open-market buying). Kevin's stated edge.
- **QD** = Lane A quality-dislocation shortlist (quality franchise sold off on sentiment/multiple, incl. post-earnings overreaction).
- **SS** = other special situation (spinoff, activist, etc.).

## Dislocation-cause tags
- **SENT** = sentiment/multiple/overreaction drop, business intact.
- **BREAK** = fundamental deterioration (margins, unit economics, guidance cut on real weakness).
- **NA** = not dislocation-sourced.

**2026-08-11 addendum.** Kevin's reasons are now on the record and they split cleanly. PLNT and SITE were passed on business-quality grounds (membership growth, business-model growth), which is exactly the judgment the first-pass exists to apply, though neither was researched before the call. LII and DINO were picked because they "fit current trends of industrials/energy," which is sector momentum, not a company reason, and is the bias #3 pattern. Track whether the trend-picked names or the stack-cleared names do better; that is the cleanest natural experiment this ledger has produced so far.

**2026-08-11 winner-attribution finding.** Kevin asked for a screen fitted to what worked (VEEV, GEHC, KMX) and against what did not (PATK). Testing the premise first: only ONE attribute separates the three winners from PATK at purchase, and it is revenue growing rather than shrinking. Piotroski runs backwards (PATK scored 8, the highest of the four; VEEV and GEHC both scored 4), beat rate runs backwards (PATK was 5/5), and drawdown runs backwards for the third time (PATK was the most dislocated at -40.6%). Taking "avoid PATK's filters" literally would have excluded CRS, the best holding in the book. Also worth recording: CRS is the real biggest winner at +113 vs SPY and Kevin left it off his own list, while the actual laggards are NBIS and IREN at about -18 each, not PATK at -1.9. n=4 over six weeks is not a factor discovery. The one filter kept from this exercise is ROIC DURABILITY (above 8% now, above 5% a year ago, at least 85% of the year-ago level), which is new and which rejected both of the names Kevin picked. See [[Winner Attribution Screen 2026-08-11]].

**2026-08-11 four-name first-pass result, and a filter defect.** Kevin said he was happy to put money into each of the four screen names. First-passes returned: LOPE dig deeper, FICO concern, WING fail, EME fail on caps. One name of four survives cleanly.

Two findings worth keeping:

1. **None of the four has a single insider purchase in twelve months.** Combined: 0 buyers, 16 sellers, ~$23.3M sold. Every one is a QD name, and the ledger baseline says INS is the primary engine and Kevin's actual edge while QD is secondary. A shortlist with zero insider buying is a shortlist with his edge switched off. Worth asking whether the winner-attribution screen should carry an insider-activity gate, or at minimum a display column, so this is visible at screen time rather than at first-pass time.

2. **The "revenue growing" filter has a defect and it fired on its first live use.** WING passed at +6.4% while domestic same-store sales fell 7.5%; the growth was 102 new franchise units. For franchisors and unit-expansion models, reported revenue is not demand. Gate on same-store or organic growth instead, or flag the model type for a manual check. Adding to [[Sourcing Rules]].
