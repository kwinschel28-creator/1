# LOPE (Grand Canyon Education) - First Pass 2026-08-11

Anchor: 146.50 (2026-08-10 close). Down 34.3% from its 12-month high. Street target 195. Forward P/E 14.3, FCF yield 6.2%.

## Verdict: DIG DEEPER. Cleanest of the four, and the cheapest

The only name of today's four with no disqualifying event, no cap breach and no fundamental break behind the drawdown.

## The numbers

Eight-quarter ROIC, rising steadily: 24.5, 24.3, 24.5, 25.4, 22.8, 23.8, 25.3, **27.2**. Not a cycle rebound, not a decay.

- Revenue growth steady and unspectacular: +7.4, +5.1, +5.3, +8.8, +9.6, +5.3, +6.7, **+6.7**. Boring in the right way, and unlike WING this is service-revenue growth, not unit-count growth.
- Operating margin 22.0%, expanding y/y from 20.9%.
- **Debt is 104M against 98M of quarterly FCF.** Effectively unlevered, D/E 0.16, current ratio 2.82.
- **Shares 29.5M to 26.5M, ~10% retired in two years**, funded from cash rather than debt. That is the buyback profile FICO does not have.
- SBC 1.3% of revenue, very low.

Piotroski swings 2 to 6 across quarters, which looks alarming until you see the pattern: it tracks the academic calendar. Q3 (Sept) prints negative FCF and low margins every year, Q4 and Q1 print high ones. Seasonality, not instability.

## The two real risks, both structural

**1. Single-customer concentration.** LOPE is a services company. It provides technology, marketing and counselling services to universities under long-term contracts, and Grand Canyon University is the overwhelming majority of revenue. This is not a diversified education business; it is one contract with one institution plus a smaller university-partner segment. That concentration is the thesis risk and it is not visible in any ratio above.

**2. Regulation.** For-profit and university-services education lives on Title IV federal student aid. Gainful-employment rules, the 90/10 rule, borrower-defence claims and periodic Department of Education rulemaking have repeatedly reset this sector's economics. LOPE has been through litigation with the DoE before. A regulatory change is the single most likely cause of permanent impairment.

Both are knowable with a few hours of reading, which is what "dig deeper" means here.
