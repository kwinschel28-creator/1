# LII (Lennox International) - First Pass 2026-08-11

Source: agent results-up/price-down screen, 2026-08-11. Kevin picked it the same day, reason given: "fits current trends of industrials/energy." That is a sector-trend reason, not a company reason. Conviction would be BORROWED plus momentum-framed.

Anchor: 423.28 (2026-08-10 close). Down ~31% from its 12-month high.

## Verdict: CONCERN

The headline numbers are the best in the book on a snapshot basis (ROIC 25.8%, ROE 67%, PE 18.5, down 31%). The trend behind every one of them is negative, and the insider file is one-directional selling. The Lane A bar is "durable high ROIC with a reinvestment runway." Durability is precisely what the eight-quarter series argues against.

## What the numbers actually show (8 quarters, Q3 2024 to Q2 2026)

**ROIC is decaying monotonically.** 36.4, 36.1, 34.9, 36.0, 35.6, 31.3, 27.4, 25.8. That is not noise, it is a ten-point slide over two years with no reversal quarter. A compounder thesis rests on the return staying high; this one is compressing every quarter.

**Debt is up ~42% while ROIC fell.** 1,423M to 2,025M. D/E 1.56. Capital went in and the return on it went down, which is the reinvestment-runway claim failing on its own evidence.

**Growth decelerated and turned choppy.** Revenue YoY by quarter: +9.6, +16.5, +2.4, +3.4, -4.8, -11.2, +5.8, +3.0. The last print is +3.0% against a company that was compounding double digits two years ago.

**Piotroski is unstable:** 7, 8, 2, 5, 6, 6, 4, 6. A quality name should not print a 2.

Genuinely positive: gross margin held 34.9%, operating margin 22.9% and expanding y/y, FCF positive, shares down 35.6M to 34.8M (~2%/yr buyback).

## Insider file: no buying, persistent selling

Twelve months of Form 4 open-market activity, non-10b5-1, tax-withholding and gifts excluded: **zero purchases, every transaction a sale.**

- Chris Kosel, VP Corporate Controller and CAO: sold 300 sh @ 591.86 (8/22/25), 254 @ 536.50 (2/4/26), then 971 @ 544.80 (5/6/26), leaving **1,090 shares**. That is near-total liquidation by the chief accounting officer, and he sold well: the stock is 423 today.
- Todd Teske, director: 600 @ 491.85 (11/11/25), 700 @ 533.45 (2/4/26).
- Karen Quintos, director: a long series of small sales @ 607-610 in Aug 2025.
- Shane Wall, director: 100 @ 540.01 (2/25/26).

Insiders sold in the 490-610 band across three quarters and the stock is now 423. The people with the best view of the ROIC decay were reducing into it.

## Fails the screen built from Kevin's own winners

LII does not survive the 2026-08-11 durability screen. ## What would change the verdict

2. Any insider buying at all, at these prices.
3. A written explanation of what the 600M of incremental debt bought.

Until then this is a decelerating cyclical at 18.5x with the CAO nearly out.
