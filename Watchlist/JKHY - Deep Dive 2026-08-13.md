## Item 2: competitive position. Structurally sound, cyclically gifted

- Core processing is a concentrated Big Three market (FIS, Fiserv, Jack Henry). JKHY runs cores for ~21% of US banks and ~12% of credit unions, focused on community institutions, and wins on service quality and a cohesive ecosystem rather than breadth.
- Switching a core is a multi-year, existential project for a bank, which is why retention is very high and revenue is recurring. The moat is churn cost, not technology.
- **The cyclical gift: both rivals are wounded.** Fiserv is sunsetting its Retail and Business Online Banking products after December 2026, forcing client decisions, and its core bank accounts are shrinking amid guidance cuts (the FISV collapse is already documented in this vault's ledger). Displaced and nervous FISV clients are JKHY's natural pipeline for exactly the community segment JKHY serves.
- The structural headwind, stated: community-bank consolidation shrinks the customer count over time, and Fiserv/FIS bundle wider product sets at larger institutions. JKHY's answer is upmarket push and per-client revenue expansion (margin guide raised to 75-95bps expansion this year). This is a mid-single-digit grower with expanding margins, not an accelerator.
