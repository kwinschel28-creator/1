## Verdict: DIG DEEPER. The inflection the screen saw is confirmed by the Q2 print; the debt is why it is cheap

Animal health (pets and farm). The bet is a leveraged turnaround finally producing: new products carrying growth while debt pays down.

## The numbers

- Revenue growth, eight quarters: -3.6, -1.4, -1.0, +4.8, +10.4, +12.2, +14.9, **+10.2**. A real acceleration, not one good quarter.
- Q2 2026 (reported 8/10): revenue $1.37B, +10% reported and +8% organic, adjusted EPS $0.34 vs $0.27 expected (+31% y/y), adjusted EBITDA +21% to $288M. **Full-year guidance RAISED** to EPS $1.10 to $1.16 on revenue $5.09B to $5.14B.
- Growth is innovation-led, which is the right kind: Zenrelia (dermatitis, the Apoquel challenger) is the largest single growth contributor, 2.5M dogs treated, first-line usage above 40%; Credelio Quattro second. Innovation revenue target raised to $1.25B for the year.
- Operating margin swing: -2.1% two years ago to +8.0% now (quarterly path is noisy from impairments and seasonality; the direction is not).
- **The caveat that keeps it Lane B: ROIC ~0.9%, D/E 0.64 on ~$4.2B of debt, GAAP still lossy TTM, shares creeping up ~1%/yr.** This is a balance-sheet recovery story priced at 20.8x forward.
