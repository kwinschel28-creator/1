## What it is / how it makes money

GE HealthCare is the imaging franchise spun from GE in January 2023: MRI, CT, ultrasound, and molecular imaging hardware, plus high-margin recurring service contracts on a huge installed base, plus pharmaceutical diagnostics (contrast and radiotracers). Razor-and-razorblade economics with regulatory and switching-cost moats. ~$22B TTM revenue `[primary]`, ~$30B market cap, #1 or #2 globally in imaging. It makes money selling the scanners, then servicing them for a decade and selling the agents that run through them.
