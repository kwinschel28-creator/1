# JKHY (Jack Henry) - First Pass 2026-08-13

## Verdict: DIG DEEPER. The durability is real; the question is only what you pay for 8% growth

Core-processing software for ~7,500 community banks and credit unions. Multi-year contracts, high switching costs, recurring revenue. This is the closest thing on the current board to Kevin's strongest footing (recurring-revenue software), wearing a Financials GICS label it does not deserve.

- Revenue growth: 4.7% to 8.7% over two years, accelerating mildly. Fiscal Q3 (reported 5/5): revenue +8.7% to $636M, EPS +12.2%.

- Shares 73.0M to 72.2M, slow retirement; buyback authorization expanded to 40M shares.
- FY26 guidance RAISED with the Q3 print (revenue ~$2.52B, EPS $6.78 to $6.87), and the stock fell anyway.

## The dislocation cause: multiple compression, not a break

The drawdown is growth-rate anxiety. Management flagged softer Q4 revenue growth and margin pressure into the second half, and the stock de-rated from a premium multiple in sympathy with a core-banking sector that FISV's implosion poisoned. Nothing in the reported numbers has broken. That is the GWRE shape: results fine, guidance intact or raised, price down.

## The three real questions

1. **The elevated-risk ruling is Kevin's to make.** GICS calls it Financials, and the Philosophy regime gates financials buys behind a deep dive. The regime's stated purpose is opaque balance sheets (banks, insurers). JKHY carries $90M of debt on a software balance sheet and holds no credit risk. The honest reading is that the regime does not apply, but that ruling has to be written by Kevin, not assumed by the agent, or it is the SLM goalpost problem in reverse.
2. **What does 8% growth deserve?** 23x forward for a single-digit grower is not a bargain; the bet is durability and margin expansion, not acceleration. If the multiple compresses to 18x, that is another 20% down with no thesis break.
3. **Earnings 2026-08-18, three sessions away.** Fiscal Q4 and FY27 guidance. The de-rating thesis gets tested immediately. Buying before the print is an event bet; the patient version waits for the 8/18 guide.
