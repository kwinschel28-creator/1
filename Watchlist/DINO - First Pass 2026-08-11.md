## Verdict: CONCERN on two independent grounds

### 1. The insider cluster is net selling once you look at both sides

The screen counted purchases only, which is what a cluster screen does. The full twelve-month open-market file, non-10b5-1, is mixed and net negative:

- Rhoman Hardy, director: 1,508 sh @ 66.32, 6/16/26, ~$100k.

**Sells, ~$1.71M / 5 sellers**
- Dale Kunneman, SVP and CHRO: 8,936 @ 84.59, **8/5/26**, ~$756k.
- Valerie Pompa, EVP Operations: 10,000 @ 69.05, 5/15/26, ~$690k.

- Vivek Garg, Acting CFO: 717 @ 71.89, 5/19/26, ~$52k.
- Manuel Fernandez, director: 635 @ 73.09, 6/2/26, ~$46k.

Net roughly -$570k. The CEO buy is real and sizeable and is the strongest single datapoint here. But **the most recent insider action is a $756k discretionary sale on 8/5, three days before the screen surfaced the name, at 84.59, near the high.** The EVP of Operations sold 10,000 shares three days before the CEO bought 15,000. This is not the XRAY setup (nine directors buying into a falling stock); it is a divided insider base.

### 2. The fundamentals are a cycle upswing, not an inflection

Refining. Eight-quarter ROIC: 3.1, 1.8, -0.4, 0.1, 4.1, 5.9, 10.6, **15.1**. Gross margin: 3.0, 1.8, 6.5, 9.2, 12.5, 5.6, 16.7, **15.1**. FCF: -313M to +1,392M. Revenue +53.2% y/y.

That is the crack-spread cycle, not a business improving. Three consequences:

- **The Piotroski 9 is a cycle artifact.** Piotroski scores year-over-year *improvement*, which is mechanically maximal coming off a trough. It is measuring the recovery, not the quality.
- **The 8x P/E and 15% FCF yield are peak-cycle multiples**, which is the classic refiner value trap: cheapest at the top of the cycle on trailing numbers.
- **Same shape as CRS**, which [[Holdings]] already flags: "clears Lane A numbers today but on PEAK-cycle margins... Lane B because active harvest is the right treatment for a cyclical at peak that he cannot explain." Adding DINO doubles down on that exact pattern, and CRS is already 2.6% at +132% on cost.

The screening workflow's own caveat applies verbatim: a single quarter of 50%+ growth after a trough is mean reversion, not a growth signal.
