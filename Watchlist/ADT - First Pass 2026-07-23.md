# ADT First Pass: 2026-07-23

## Triage: Dig deeper
A genuine 5-insider cluster (incl. CFO and a director) in a cash-generative, sticky recurring-revenue business that is cheap (~$6.90), strategically backed by State Farm and Google, and fully outside the AI/data-center theme. The two things to resolve are the $7.7B debt load and a possible State Farm share-registration overhang. This is the strongest of the four screened diversifiers on quality.

## What it is / how it makes money
Home security and smart-home monitoring, sold as multi-year subscription contracts, so revenue is recurring and switching costs are real (installed hardware plus monitoring relationship) `[secondary]`. In 2026 State Farm took ~15% equity (~$1.2B) and committed up to $300M for product; Google (already ~6% since 2020) added $150M `[secondary]`. CEO Devries turnaround; installed-base cash cow, not a grower.

- Unexplainable business: clear.
