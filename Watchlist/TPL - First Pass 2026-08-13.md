# TPL (Texas Pacific Land) - First Pass 2026-08-13

Anchor: 342.77 (2026-08-12 close). Down 37.4% from its 52-week high of ~540 (March 2026). Street target 443.50. Forward P/E 39.6, trailing 43.7. Dividend 0.66%. Short interest 9.1%.

## Verdict: CONCERN. A great business at a price that still assumes it, with three unforced problems

The best balance sheet on the board (D/E 0.01, current ratio 4.55, 78% operating margins, revenue +31%) attached to the wrong setup: decaying returns, negative free cash flow, a key-shareholder overhang, and a 40x forward multiple for oil-price torque.

## The numbers

- Operating margin 73% to 78%, revenue +31% y/y, essentially no debt. The royalty model is intact.
- **ROIC decaying monotonically: 38.7, 37.1, 35.4, 34.2, 32.7, 32.3, 32.6** over eight quarters. Still elite, but the direction is the LII/AZO shape the winner-attribution filter exists to catch, and at 40x forward the direction matters more than the level.
- **TTM free cash flow is NEGATIVE (FCF yield -0.3%),** extraordinary for a royalty company. The cause is a heavy capital program (land, minerals and water infrastructure including desalination) plus an EBITDA miss in Q1 where expenses grew faster than revenue. The company that historically converted nearly all revenue to cash currently converts none of it.
- Piotroski noisy (2 to 7 across quarters).

## The dislocation cause: three stacked, none clean

1. **Oil.** OPEC+ added supply from June 2026; crude weakness flows straight through a royalty model. This is macro torque, not company failure, but it is also not a mispricing you have an edge on.
2. **Execution.** Q1 adjusted EBITDA missed by 11% with margin contraction. For a company whose thesis is operating leverage on royalties, expense growth outpacing revenue is a real strike.
3. **The Stahl overhang.** Board member Murray Stahl, CEO of Horizon Kinetics, TPL's largest shareholder, passed away in April 2026. Horizon Kinetics' position is enormous relative to float; any estate or fund restructuring is a standing supply overhang that no fundamental work resolves.

## Insider file

Six months, both sides: 2 buyers ($0.51M, one fresh on 8/11) against $1.27M discretionary selling. Mixed, leaning negative. The fresh buy is the only genuinely encouraging line.

## Lane logic, and why it fails both

- Lane A wants a durable compounder bought on dislocation: the ROIC direction and the FCF inversion fail the durability leg today.
- Lane B wants an insider cluster or a defined special situation: the cluster is not there (net selling) and the "situation" (capex program maturing into cash flow, overhang clearing) has no date and no defined payoff line.

Data-center and water optionality on Permian acreage is real but it is a narrative until it shows up in segment numbers, and narrative-plus-multiple is the OKLO refusal shape at 40x.

## What would change the verdict

FCF turning positive as the capex program completes, the Horizon Kinetics position resolving, or a genuine insider cluster forming. Any of the three reopens it; the price alone does not.

Process gate: first-pass only. Watchlist.
