# EME (EMCOR Group) - First Pass 2026-08-11

Anchor: 809.33 (2026-08-10 close). Down 15.0% from its 12-month high. Street target 1,044.

## Verdict: FAIL, and it is a rule, not a judgment call

**EMCOR's growth is the AI/data-center theme.** Confirmed from the Q2 2026 reporting: data-centre revenues inside the network and communications sector rose 45-50% year over year and accounted for roughly **two-thirds of the entire US electrical division's revenue growth**. Record RPOs of $17.14B. FY2026 guidance was raised explicitly on data-centre demand.

Under [[Portfolio Strategy]] a theme is a shared return driver, not a sector label. EMCOR's incremental dollar of revenue and its backlog are driven by the same AI-capex cycle that drives GOOG, ORCL, NBIS and IREN. That group is at **~55.0% against a 30% hard cap**, already roughly double the limit.

The new-money rule is explicit: no add that pushes a theme past its hard cap. The theme is already past it. So EME is not buyable on the current book regardless of how good the company is, and it would make the single worst number in the portfolio worse while looking like diversification because it is labelled Industrials.

This is the exact trap the theme cap exists to catch. Sector labels say EME diversifies; the mechanism says it concentrates.

## For the record, the business is excellent

Eight-quarter series is the strongest of any name looked at today.

- ROIC rising: 29.2, 30.8, 30.0, 29.8, 29.9, 32.1, 31.2, **31.7**.
- Revenue growth accelerating: +9.6, +12.7, +17.4, +16.4, +19.7, +19.7, **+19.8**.
- Debt tiny and stable at 542M, D/E 0.13.
- Shares 46.7M to 44.4M, ~5% retired in two years.
- **SBC is 0.1% of revenue.** Almost unheard of; management is not paying itself in stock.
- Piotroski 7, operating margin 10.6% and expanding.

If the theme cap were not breached this would be the best Lane A candidate surfaced in weeks.

## Insider file: zero buys, heavy selling

Twelve months, open-market, non-10b5-1: **0 buyers, 8 sellers, ~$14.56M sold.** Last sale 2026-06-17. No insider has bought a share at any price.

## What would change the verdict

Not a company event. A portfolio event: the AI/data-center theme getting back under 30%, which on the current arithmetic requires the GOOG plan running to completion **and** a written revision of the theme definition, because GOOG and ORCL alone are ~56% of the theme. That decision is still unmade and is logged in [[Holdings]].

Alternatively, a written argument that construction-services exposure to data centres is a different return driver from owning the compute itself. That argument is available and might even be right, since EMCOR gets paid on contracted backlog rather than on GPU rental rates. But it has to be written down and it changes the theme definition for every other name, so it is not a one-line exception for a name Kevin wants today.
