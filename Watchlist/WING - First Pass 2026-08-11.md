# WING (Wingstop) - First Pass 2026-08-11

Anchor: 117.52 (2026-08-10 close). Down 66.0% from its 12-month high.

## Verdict: FAIL, and the screen that surfaced it was wrong

**Domestic same-store sales fell 7.5% in Q2 2026, and on 7/29 the company cut full-year comp guidance to -4% to -6%**, citing sustained pressure on lower-income consumers. Digital visits in urban trade areas, where over 55% of restaurants sit, fell 9%.

This is the pattern the vault already flagged on 2026-08-10: a deep-drawdown screen "selects almost perfectly for companies that cut guidance in the last month." WING is -66% off its high *and* cut guidance thirteen days ago. It belongs with FISV, PODD and CHRW, all killed on their forward outlook.

## Why my screen let it through, and the fix

The screen required revenue growth above +3%. WING printed **+6.4%**. That revenue growth is **franchise unit growth, not demand**: the company opened 102 restaurants in the quarter and reiterated 15-16% global unit growth, so royalty and franchise-fee revenue rose while the underlying restaurants sold 7.5% less.

**A franchisor's reported revenue can rise while every existing store declines.** My "revenue growing" filter, which came out of the winner-attribution exercise this morning, does not distinguish demand growth from unit growth. That is a real defect and it appeared on the filter's first live use.

Fix to carry into [[Sourcing Rules]]: for franchise and unit-expansion models, gate on same-store or organic growth, not reported revenue. Where comps are unavailable in the database, the name needs a manual check before it can pass.

## The other numbers

Not all bad, which is what makes it dangerous. ROIC has been rising: 26.6, 20.9, 21.5, 22.4, 23.9, 27.6, **29.6**. Operating margin 29.4% and expanding. Gross margin 86.5%, stable.

But: debt 1,272M against a franchisor's small asset base, FCF was **-11M** last quarter, Piotroski 5, and **short interest is 17% of float**, the highest of the four names screened. Street target 209 against a 117.52 close is a 78% gap, which usually means estimates have not been cut yet rather than that the stock is cheap.

## Insider file

Twelve months, open-market, non-10b5-1: **0 buyers, 3 sellers, ~$986k sold.** Last sale 2026-05-12. Nobody inside is buying a 66% decline.

## What would change the verdict

Two consecutive quarters of domestic comps stabilising, and any insider buying. Both are checkable and neither has happened. Until then the drawdown is a fundamental break, tagged BREAK not SENT.

Process gate: first-pass only. Recommend dropping from the shortlist.
