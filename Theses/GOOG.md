# GOOG: Thesis

> Documented 2026-06-30 from Kevin's own reasoning. This is the only core holding sourced from his own judgment rather than a tip. Lane A, conviction compounder.

## One-line thesis
Alphabet was undervalued in summer 2025 when "weak AI" fear compressed its multiple, even though it owns several compounding revenue streams (Search, YouTube, Cloud) plus real optionality (Waymo, Gemini). Buy the franchise while the market mistook an AI threat for an AI death sentence.

## How they make money
Search and YouTube advertising are the profit core. Google Cloud is the second engine. Waymo and Gemini are optionality, not yet load-bearing for the cash flows.

## The three load-bearing claims
1. The Search and ad franchise stays durable: AI assistants (ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Gemini itself) do not structurally erode query volume or ad pricing. The "weak AI" fear was wrong, not just early.
2. Cloud and AI become a real, growing profit engine, with the AI capex earning a return rather than being a subsidy.
3. The optionality (Waymo and the rest) is roughly free at the price paid.

## Why the edge persists (moat)
Search distribution, data scale, and habit; YouTube's scale and creator network; Cloud scale economics. The question to keep testing is whether AI changes the distribution layer that the Search moat rests on.

## Valuation (watch this closely)
Bought on a depressed multiple during the summer 2025 AI scare. As of 2026-06-30, GOOG trades near $350.80 at roughly a 25.5 P/E, up about 89% from cost. The cheap-multiple part of the thesis has largely closed: this is no longer an undervaluation bet, it is now a compounding bet at a market multiple. That shift is the single most important thing to be honest about.

## What would make me WRONG (pre-registered disconfirmers)
- AI search measurably erodes Google Search query volume or ad pricing for two or more quarters (the original fear proving right, just late).
- Cloud growth or margin reverses, or AI capex visibly fails to earn a return.
- An adverse antitrust remedy hits the ad or search business (ongoing cases).

## Monitoring triggers
- Search revenue growth decelerating for two consecutive quarters, or paid-click and pricing deterioration.
- Google Cloud operating margin rolling over.
- A material antitrust ruling or remedy.
- Position-size trigger (portfolio, not thesis): GOOG is 56% of the book and 100% of the Roth, far above the 20% single-name cap. This is a risk flag independent of whether the business thesis holds.
