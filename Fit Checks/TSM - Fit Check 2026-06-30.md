# TSM Portfolio Fit: 2026-06-30

## Fit: Concentrating (looks like diversification, is the same bet)

TSM feels like diversification because it is a different sector, semiconductors rather than software or ads. It is not. Its demand driver is identical to the bet that is already about 87% of your book: AI and data-center capex. HPC is roughly 61% of TSM revenue and 2026 guidance is for more than 30% revenue growth on AI demand, with capex pushed to $52 to $56B [secondary, SCMP / earnings coverage, Apr to May 2026]. Adding TSM is a third expression of the AI-infrastructure bet wearing a different costume, and it bolts on Taiwan geopolitical and currency risk while sitting outside your strongest circle.

## Overlap with current book

- **Hidden correlation, the heart of this check.** GOOG (cloud and AI), ORCL (AI data-center capex), and TSM (the foundry making the AI chips for Nvidia, AMD, and Apple) all move with one thing: AI infrastructure spend. TSM is the picks-and-shovels layer underneath the very names you already own. When the AI capex cycle turns, all three turn together. Your theme cap exists precisely to catch "several positions that are really the same bet" [vault].
- **Theme load.** Pure AI / data-center-capex exposure, added to a theme already at about 87% versus a 30% cap [vault].
- **What it genuinely adds (but these are risks, not return-diversifiers):** TWD/USD currency exposure and a Taiwan/China geopolitical tail. New, yes; diversifying in the good sense, no.

## Concentration check

- **Theme:** GOOG + ORCL = 87.3% of the book [estimate, Holdings 2026-06-30]. TSM is essentially 100% this theme, so your new-money rule blocks it outright: "No add that pushes a theme past its hard cap" [vault].
- **Entry discipline:** TSM trades near its fifty-two-week high (~$469 against a $221 to $477 range, P/E ~36x, market cap ~$1.9T) [secondary, multiple quote sources, Jun 30 2026]. That is the opposite of dislocation, so it also fails your Lane A entry gate of buying quality only when it is dislocated [vault].

## Sizing under their rules (not advice)

- It does not clear the theme gate, so there is no compliant size. If it did, Lane A starter would be 2 to 3% (~$410 to $615) [vault]. Out-of-strongest-circle means your own rule caps size at the low end and requires a full deep dive with no fast-track [vault].
- Funding: only $275 cash, so buying TSM means trimming GOOG or ORCL, which is again selling AI to buy AI.

## Conflicts / constraints

- **Your biases, your words.** Bias #4 all-in and bias #3 theme-as-thesis [vault]. If the pull is "AI is the future," that is the theme standing in for a thesis, the same machinery that produced your BRBR loss.
- **Your Philosophy:** no informational edge in liquid large caps; TSM is among the largest in the world [vault]. And the per-business test: can you sketch on one page how a leading-edge foundry earns its returns AND model the Taiwan tail? If not, it is out, whatever the sector [vault].
- **Fit is not merit.** TSM is a superb business. This note is only saying it does not fit this book; it deepens the one risk you are already most exposed to.
