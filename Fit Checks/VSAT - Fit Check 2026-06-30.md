# VSAT Portfolio Fit: 2026-06-30

## Fit: Additive (the only one of the three that reduces your AI concentration), with circle and survivability caveats

VSAT is the only candidate here that actually diversifies your book. Its return drivers, satellite and space, defense, in-flight connectivity, and broadband, have nothing to do with the AI / data-center-capex theme that is about 87% of your money, so on pure portfolio fit it is additive. The honest catch is that fit is not merit: VSAT sits outside your strongest circle, it is a leveraged turnaround (net debt about 3.1x), and it shares a defense and aerospace driver with the CRS sliver you already want to cut.

## Overlap with current book

- **Theme:** satellite communications plus defense plus aviation and maritime broadband. Zero overlap with the GOOG/ORCL AI-capex theme. This is exactly the breadth your book lacks.
- **Minor hidden correlation, flagged honestly.** VSAT's Defense and Advanced Technologies segment (double-digit growth, recent Space Force contract wins) [secondary, Jun 2026] shares a defense/aerospace demand driver with CRS (specialty alloys, aerospace/defense). CRS is only 3% of the book and you have flagged it for cutting, so the overlap is immaterial today; just do not treat both as independent "defense diversification," they lean on the same driver.
- **New factor exposures (welcome here):** defense budgets, air-travel recovery, the LEO-versus-GEO competitive battle, and the satellite capex cycle.

## Concentration check

- A starter VSAT touches no theme cap, and if funded by trimming GOOG or ORCL it actually reduces your AI concentration. This is the rare add that helps two problems at once: it diversifies, and its funding trim chips at your biggest breach.
- Single-name: a 2 to 3% starter is far under any ceiling [vault].
